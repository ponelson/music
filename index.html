01 / Sound recording
The master

The recorded performance. It may be owned or licensed by an artist, label, distributor, catalog buyer or financing vehicle. Interactive-service payments usually travel through negotiated master licenses.

02 / Musical work
The composition

The song itself. Songwriters and publishers participate through performance and mechanical systems, with territory, society affiliation, ownership and administration affecting the route.

The rule behind every calculator

There is no universal major-service “per-stream rate.” The tools below use visible, editable assumptions to estimate attributable revenue pools and contractual cash flows. Statements and contracts remain controlling.

Control sits across labels, publishers and capital.

1997 to today

From royalty bonds to permanent capital.

Bowie kept ownership and borrowed against future royalties. The next cycle added outright copyright purchases, platform take-privates, private credit, ABS and operating-company consolidation.

1997

Bowie Bonds

$55 million, 10-year securities at 7.9%, backed by royalties from 25 pre-1990 David Bowie albums.

2018

Hipgnosis Songs Fund launches

A London-listed vehicle made song catalogs legible as an institutional income asset.

2020

Bob Dylan / UMPG

Universal Music Publishing Group acquired Dylan’s entire songwriting catalog.

2020

Stevie Nicks / Primary Wave

A majority stake in publishing copyrights and related interests joined Primary Wave’s active-management platform.

2021

Blackstone + Hipgnosis

The parties announced a $1 billion partnership for songs, recordings, music IP and royalties.

2021

Bruce Springsteen / Sony

Sony acquired both the recorded-music and songwriting catalogs in a landmark whole-of-rights transaction.

2022

Sting / UMPG

UMPG acquired Sting’s songwriting catalog across solo work and The Police repertoire.

2022

Genesis interests / Concord

Concord acquired musical works and recordings interests associated with Tony Banks, Phil Collins and Mike Rutherford.

2023

Justin Bieber / Hipgnosis

Publishing, artist royalties and neighboring-rights interests through 2021 reportedly sold for about $200 million.

2023

Katy Perry / Litmus

Litmus acquired catalog interests covering Perry’s releases from 2008 through 2020.

2023

Concord prices $1.8B ABS

A large music-rights securitization financed corporate growth and demonstrated catalog-credit scale.

2024

Michael Jackson estate / Sony

Sony reportedly acquired half of Jackson’s publishing and recorded-music interests for at least $600 million.

2024

KISS / Pophouse

Pophouse acquired the music catalog, brand name and intellectual property of KISS.

2024

Queen / Sony

A reported $1.27 billion transaction became the largest widely reported artist-catalog deal.

2024

Hipgnosis taken private

Blackstone completed the acquisition of Hipgnosis Songs Fund and later consolidated branding under Recognition Music Group.

2024

Legends acquires ASM Global

Venue management, premium experiences and commercial services consolidated into a broader live-events platform.

2025

Taylor Swift repurchases masters

Swift acquired the masters to her first six albums from Shamrock Capital.

2025

Music ABS expands

Blackstone led a landmark securitization backed by the Hipgnosis portfolio, extending the debt-market model.

2026

BMG and Concord combine

The combination completed in September; Apollo announced a $1.25 billion capital solution supporting the transaction and portfolio.

Ownership, operation and geometry

The building owner may not run the show.

Public authorities, universities and teams often own the real estate. Private operators manage the venue; promoters book shows; ticketing and hospitality may sit with still other companies.

Concert layout

Stage

End-stage

Most floor and bowl seats face one end. It maximizes production control but kills or discounts seats behind the stage.

Sellable capacity is not fire-code capacity. Stage footprint, sightlines, production kills, premium inventory, ADA holds and local rules determine the seats available for a specific show.
